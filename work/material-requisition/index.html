Material & Purchase Requisition

A set of custom Odoo 16 modules that takes a material request from creation through approval, stock check and issue from stores, and carries anything short through purchase requisition, RFQ, PO approval and receipt, with dashboards that show each team what is late.

Role
Developer, end to end
Company
T&I Projects Private Limited
Platform
Odoo 16 Enterprise
Scope
Requests to purchase, with dashboards

The problem

Material requests were handled manually rather than through one structured flow in Odoo. That made it harder to see who had approved a request, to check stock before issuing material, to pass shortages on to purchasing consistently, and to see where any document was waiting.

What I built

  • Material requests as structured documents, with requester, department and business references.
  • Approval workflow: Production Manager approval, then inventory review, with personal, login-protected approval links in the e-mails.
  • Stock check and material issue through real stock transfers, including partial issue and returns.
  • Purchase requisitions for shortages, approved by a director, then vendor quotes, RFQ and PO approval by order amount.
  • Receipt and issue back to the requester once goods arrive.
  • Management dashboard for directors and managers: requests raised and issued, request → issue and PR → PO times, shortages, and a process flow of where documents wait, by period and department.
  • Product variants through the TI Variant Configurator, so requests name the exact variant.

How it works

  1. RequestA production user answers a short Request Materials pop-up and submits it.
  2. ManagerThe Production Manager approves, from Odoo or the e-mail link.
  3. StoresInventory reviews availability and issues what is in stock.
  4. ShortageShort items go to a director. Once approved, a purchase requisition is created and sent to Purchase automatically.
  5. PurchaseVendor quotes, RFQ, and PO approval by order amount.
  6. Receive & issueGoods are received and issued to the requester.

Try it

Both screens are redrawn from the modules with sample data, so you can click through them. In the first, change the request in the pop-up, submit it and move it step by step. In the second, the Management dashboard directors and managers open: change the period, open a tab, or open it as another role.
