Export Packing Customization

Export packing list and packing slip PDF reports rebuilt in QWeb to match the business's own export document template, with configuration options for the people who print them.

Role
Developer
Company
T&I Projects
Platform
Odoo 16 QWeb

The problem

Export shipments need packing documents in the business's own format. The standard Odoo layouts didn't match it, so documents needed manual preparation outside the ERP.

What I built

  • Export packing list and packing slip QWeb PDF reports.
  • Layouts matched to the business document template, with package-level details and product lines.
  • Configurable report fields to choose what is printed.
  • Logo display setting.
  • Port and package-type selection.

How it works

  1. PreparePacking data is recorded on the shipment in Odoo.
  2. ConfigureThe user selects port, package type, fields and logo display.
  3. PrintOdoo renders the packing list or packing slip as a PDF.
