Export Packing: Packing List and Packing Slip

An Odoo 16 module that turns an export delivery into the Packing List (PL) and one Packing Slip (PS) sticker per package, in the company's EX053 layout, with weights calculated as you type.

Role
Developer
Company
T&I Projects Private Limited
Platform
Odoo 16 QWeb

The problem

The export team typed packing lists in Word for every shipment: a goods summary, then every box with its contents and weights, then a sticker per package. Quantities and weights were copied by hand, and a mistake meant redoing the whole document.

What I built

  • A Packing List tab on every delivery, in four steps: header, page 1 goods summary, page 2 packing details, then check and print.
  • Fill Export Defaults fills only the empty header fields from the customer, the sales order and its invoice. Fetch Goods brings page 1 up to the delivered quantities.
  • Add all products fills page 2 from each product's packing data: packing type, size, quantity per package, weight and gross %.
  • Page 2 divides page 1, never more: a row can't pack more than page 1, and the rest goes to the next row. Yellow warnings show what is still to pack.
  • Contents per package: a box of mixed spares lists every item with its quantity, with no product needed.
  • Net = Qty × Unit Wt and Gross = Net + Gross %, as you type. Long packages continue over the next page with "(continued)".
  • Print PL and Print Packing Slip: QWeb PDF reports in the company's export layout.

How it works

  1. HeaderFill Export Defaults, then check consignee, shipping mark, ports and vessel.
  2. Page 1Fetch Goods: one line per product with its delivered quantity.
  3. Page 2Add all products, or one row per package group, with contents and weights.
  4. PrintRead the warnings, check the totals, then Print PL or Print Packing Slip.

Try it

Redrawn from the current module (16.0.8.1.1) with sample data. Press the buttons in order, change a Qty (try more than page 1) or a Gross %, then print the Packing List or flip through the Packing Slip stickers.

Versions

More than 20 versions as the export team asked for changes, each with an upgrade script, a tagged commit and a changelog entry, and each checked on a copy of the live database before it went live (page 2, stickers and totals the same).
