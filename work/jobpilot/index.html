JobPilot

A job-search automation product I designed and built on my own. It runs locally, so the user keeps control of their data, and nothing is ever submitted on their behalf.

Problem

An active job search means checking many sources, judging fit again and again, tailoring a CV for each role and remembering where every application stands. I wanted a tool that does this systematically, explains its judgments and keeps personal data on the user's own machine.

Solution

  • Job discovery from permitted sources (public job boards and company ATS feeds), Gmail job alerts and a browser extension.
  • Relevance score out of 100 with a reason for every point, plus duplicate detection and checks that job links are still live.
  • Resume and cover letter tailoring per job (DOCX and PDF) from one master profile, with a review step before anything is used.
  • Application tracking from saved to offer, with follow-ups and interview preparation.
  • MCP server so AI assistants such as Claude can work with the user's job data.
  • Local execution: a local database, optional local AI, and no automatic submissions.

My contribution

Everything: product design, data model, FastAPI backend, server-rendered interface, scoring rules, document generation, Gmail connector, MCP server, browser extension and test suite.

Functional workflow

  1. ProfileThe user keeps one master profile of facts.
  2. DiscoverJobs arrive from sources, alerts or the extension.
  3. ScoreEach job is scored out of 100 with reasons.
  4. DecideSave it, or reject with a reason the tool learns from.
  5. TailorGenerate and review a tailored CV and letter.
  6. TrackApply manually, then track and follow up.
