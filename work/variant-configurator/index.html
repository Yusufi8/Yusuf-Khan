T&I Projects · Odoo 16

Variant Configurator with purchase prices

Odoo 16 modules that let an administrator choose which screens pick the exact product variant, and show what each variant costs right in the pop-up: this vendor's price, the last price paid, other vendors and open RFQs.

Role
Developer
Company
T&I Projects Private Limited
Platform
Odoo 16

The problem

Products such as bolts, motors and pipes come in many variants (size, length, finish). Several standard Odoo screens pick a product, not the exact variant, and a vendor price set for the whole product was offered for every variant: a 15 HP price for a 5 HP motor. Buyers could not see what a variant had cost before without searching old orders.

What I built

  • Variant Selection Rules (Manufacturing › Configuration): an administrator switches any product field to Variant Mode or back to Normal Mode, on one view or on every view, for chosen groups or companies, with no code per screen. A Coverage box lists every view where the rule applies or was skipped.
  • Shipped rules for manufacturing orders (product, components and by-products), and switched-off examples for transfers and scrap. Bills of Materials, material requests and the Request Materials pop-up use registered adapters.
  • In Variant Mode, typing lists the product once (Hex Bolt (10 variants)), and choosing it opens the configurator with the line's quantity. Combinations that don't exist can't be confirmed.
  • A purchase price panel in the pop-up: this vendor's price list entry, last bought (net price, vendor, date, PO), up to 3 other vendors and open RFQs, updated as the variant changes. Shown to the purchase team only.
  • Vendor prices per variant: a variant's own price wins over that vendor's price for the whole product, and confirmed orders record the price for the variant bought.
  • Last Purchase Price (Variant) on each variant, and an alert on RFQ lines when the price moves past a set percentage (5% by default).

How it works

  1. Set upThe administrator switches a screen's product field to Variant Mode with a rule.
  2. PickOn that screen, the user types the product and chooses size, length and finish in the pop-up.
  3. Check the priceThe panel shows this vendor's price, the last price paid, other vendors and open RFQs.
  4. ConfirmThe exact variant and quantity go on the line; Odoo's own onchanges run as usual.

Try it

Redrawn from the current modules with sample data. Open Transfer and type "bolt": with the rule off, Odoo lists every variant. Switch Transfers - Operations on in Variant Selection Rules and type again. In Purchase, pick variants and watch the prices change; Product variant shows its vendor prices and last purchase.
