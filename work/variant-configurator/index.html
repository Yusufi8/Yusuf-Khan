TI Variant Configurator

An Odoo 16 customization that lets Purchase, Bills of Materials and Material Requisition select the exact product variant, where the standard flows select at product level.

Business problem

Several standard Odoo flows select products at product (template) level. The business needed purchase orders, bills of materials and material requisitions to name the specific variant, so the right item is bought, built and issued.

Solution

  • Adapted an existing product configurator for variant-level selection.
  • Enabled it in Purchase, Bills of Materials and Material Requisition.
  • Downstream documents carry the selected variant.

My contribution

  • Adapted the configurator's models and views for the three flows.
  • Integrated it with the Material & Purchase Requisition workflow.
  • Tested the end-to-end flows before handing it over.

Functional workflow

  1. Open documentA user works on a PO, BoM or material requisition.
  2. ConfigureThey pick the product and configure the variant.
  3. ResolveThe matching variant is placed on the line.
  4. ContinueStandard purchasing, manufacturing or issue flows use that variant.
