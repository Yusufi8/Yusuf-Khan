T&I Projects Private Limited · Odoo 16 Enterprise

Material & Purchase Requisition

Workers ask for material from a short pop-up. The Production Manager approves, stores check stock and issue it, and anything short goes to a director. Once approved, a purchase requisition is created and sent to Purchase automatically. Every step is recorded with who did it and when.

  • Request, approval, stores issue and purchase in one flow
  • Approve or refuse straight from the e-mail
  • Two paths: all in stock, or short and through purchase

Try itIn the pop-up, press Submit Request and move the request step by step: the Welding Rod is short, so it takes the director path. Set its Qty to 8 or less and it goes straight to the issue.

T&I Projects Private Limited · Odoo 16

Variant Configurator with purchase prices

An administrator decides which pages pick the exact product variant. A Variant Selection Rule switches a product field (on one view or on every view) to Variant Mode, with no code per screen. The pop-up then shows what the variant costs: this vendor's price, the last price paid, other vendors and open RFQs.

  • Rules for transfers, manufacturing orders, scrap and more
  • Vendor prices per variant, not one price for all
  • Last purchase price and an alert when an RFQ price jumps

Try itOpen Transfer and type "bolt": every variant is listed. Then in Variant Selection Rules switch Transfers - Operations on and try again. In Purchase, pick variants and watch the prices change.

T&I Projects Private Limited · Odoo 16

Export Packing: Packing List and Packing Slip

The export team used to type packing lists in Word. Now a delivery has a Packing List tab in four steps, and Odoo prints the Packing List (PL) and one Packing Slip (PS) sticker per package in the company's export layout, with weights calculated as you type.

  • Page 2 can divide page 1, never pack more
  • Boxes of mixed spares with their contents
  • Every version checked on a copy of the live database first

Try itPress Fill Export Defaults, Fetch Goods and Add all products. Change a Qty or Gross %, then Print PL or Print Packing Slip.

T&I Projects Private Limited · Odoo 16 · OWL

Management Dashboard

One page for directors and managers: what was done in a period and what is open now, per area. Each role sees only its own tabs, every number can be compared with another period, and every number opens the records behind it. Facts only, no ranking of people.

  • Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Manufacturing, Requisitions and Process flow
  • Filters by period, department and employee, also on shared logins
  • Each tab is read with the user's own access rights

Try itChange the Period or Compare with, open a tab, press a ?. Then open it as a Purchase or Sales Manager and see the tabs change.

T&I Projects Private Limited · Odoo 16

People Tracking with Login PIN

On the shop floor several people share one Odoo login. Each person taps their name and enters their own 4-digit Login PIN, so every step is saved under the real person, and the others on that login do not see their documents. Five wrong PINs lock the name until an administrator resets it.

  • Names on every step and in every chatter line
  • Managers on their own login are never asked
  • Work by Person and Activities reports

Try itPick User 1 (PIN 1234) and press Request. Tap the name at the top, switch to User 3 and set a new PIN. Try a wrong PIN five times.
