T&I Projects Private Limited · Odoo 16

People Tracking with Login PIN

On the shop floor several people share one Odoo login. Each person taps their name and enters their own 4-digit Login PIN, so every step is saved under the real person, and the others on that login do not see their documents. Five wrong PINs lock the name until an administrator resets it.

  • Names on every step and in every chatter line
  • Managers on their own login are never asked
  • Work by Person and Activities reports

How I work

From the first conversation to people using it

I talk to the people who do the work

Before any code, I sit with the users, follow a document through the real process, and note what standard Odoo already does well.

You get: a clear picture of the process and what really needs building

A simple plan we agree on

The flow, the screens, the approvals and the reports, drawn out and agreed before I build, so there are no surprises.

You get: a workflow diagram and a scope you can sign off

Clean, upgrade-friendly modules

Custom modules in Python, XML and OWL that extend standard Odoo instead of fighting it, with upgrade scripts for every version.

You get: modules in Git, with a changelog

Tested by code, then by your users

Automated tests run before every release (more than 580 across my T&I modules), then your team tries it in a UAT copy with step-by-step test sheets.

You get: test results and UAT sheets

A rehearsed go-live

A full backup, a rehearsal on a copy of your real database, version checks and a written rollback plan. Then the real install, and checks after it.

You get: a release package with a start-here guide and checklist

People who know how to use it

Simple user guides and training in English, Tamil or Hindi, and quick fixes after launch. Ongoing support can be arranged however suits you.

You get: trained users and someone to call
